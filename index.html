Minutes 1–7
We build your clinical picture
Teos responds in under a minute per turn, following clinical reasoning — gathering your history, medications, and risk factors across multiple rounds.
I need to understand this better. Can you describe the pain — is it burning, cramping, or sharp? Point to where it's worst.
Burning pain, mostly in my upper stomach. Sometimes it radiates up to my chest.
Any blood in your vomit or stool? Are you taking NSAIDs, aspirin, or any other medications? Any recent weight loss?
No blood. I take ibuprofen for back pain a few times a week. I've lost maybe 5 pounds.
3
Minutes 8–18
Deep clinical intake
Targeted follow-up questions across ~14 turns — diet, stress, family history, alarm symptoms — building the kind of thorough history that gets rushed in a 5-minute office visit.
A few more questions to narrow this down. Any family history of stomach ulcers or GI cancers? How much alcohol do you drink per week? Any recent travel?
My dad had an ulcer in his 40s. I drink maybe 4-5 beers on weekends. No travel.
Given what you've shared, I have enough to provide an initial assessment. Let me pull this together.
