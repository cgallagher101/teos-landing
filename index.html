Describe what's going on
Open Teos and describe your symptoms in plain language. No forms, no phone trees, no hold music.

2
Minutes 1–7
We build your clinical picture
Teos responds in under a minute per turn, following clinical reasoning — gathering your history, medications, and risk factors across multiple rounds.
I need to understand this better. Can you describe the pain — is it burning, cramping, or sharp? Point to where it's worst.
Burning pain, mostly in my upper stomach. It gets worse after eating and sometimes wakes me up at night.
Any blood in your vomit or stool? Are you taking NSAIDs, aspirin, or any other medications?
No blood. I take ibuprofen for back pain a few times a week.
3
Minutes 8–18
Deep clinical intake
A real conversation, not a form — as thorough as it needs to be. Diet, stress, family history, alarm symptoms — building the kind of thorough history that gets rushed in a 5-minute office visit.
A few more questions to narrow this down. Any family history of stomach ulcers or GI cancers? How much alcohol do you drink per week?
My dad had an ulcer in his 40s. I drink maybe 4-5 beers on weekends.
Given what you've shared, I have enough to provide an initial assessment. Let me pull this together.
